About Daksh Lab

Daksh Lab was founded with a simple promise to the people of Bagalkot: every report should be accurate, on time, and easy to understand.

Every sample is processed on calibrated, fully automated analyzers and checked under daily internal quality control. Results are reviewed and signed by qualified MD pathologists before they reach you.

We know waiting for results is stressful. That's why most routine reports are delivered the same day on WhatsApp and email. Our home collection service makes testing easier for elderly parents, expecting mothers and busy families.
